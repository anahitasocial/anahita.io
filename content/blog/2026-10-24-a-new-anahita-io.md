For years, anahita.io was itself an Anahita site, running the PHP version
that came before today's. It has been retired, and this website takes its
place.

## What Anahita is now

Anahita has been rebuilt. Since March 2023 it has been Anahita Services,
written in Go, and Anahita Web, a web app in React, and it speaks two languages of the
open social web: ActivityPub, which Mastodon and Pixelfed speak, and the AT
Protocol of Bluesky.

It is made for organisations that want a social network of their own:
schools, newsrooms, libraries, research groups and non-profits. People,
groups, events, articles, photos and videos, in one install, with a
chronological feed and nothing tracking anybody.

## What this site is for

1. To say plainly what Anahita is, and who it is for.
2. To point to the documentation, which lives with the code.
3. To tell its history, which goes back further than most of the fediverse.
4. To carry news, here on the blog.

This site practises what Anahita promises: it has no analytics, no cookies,
and asks nothing of any other server while you read it. Even its search runs
in your browser.

## What comes next

Anahita Services will be published as open source, and the docs and the API
reference will open with them. After that comes banu.io, a public Anahita
site.
