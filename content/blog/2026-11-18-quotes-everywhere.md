Quoting a post, with a few words added above it, is one way conversations spread. Anahita has had quotes for a while, and authors can choose who can quote them: anyone, their followers, or no one.  However, until now, quotes that left Anahita for the fediverse or Bluesky appeared as posts with links.  Starting with Anahita Services v1.3.3, quotes now arrive as quotes themselves.

## On Mastodon

Mastodon’s quotes are built on consent, and so are Anahita’s, making them a perfect fit.  When you quote a post from Mastodon, Anahita first checks with the author’s server. If they approve, the post you quoted appears inside yours.  If they decline, your post goes out without the quote.

This system works the other way around as well.  If someone on Mastodon requests to quote a post written here, Anahita responds according to the author’s preference: yes for everyone, yes for their followers only, or no.

![A quote on Mastodon: Rastin Mehr's post about Anahita channels, from banu.io, with the Anahita.io channel's post and its picture shown inside it](/images/blog/quote-repost-mastodon.webp "On Mastodon")

## On Bluesky

On Bluesky, quoting a post displays the quoted post, including any accompanying pictures, within your post.  Quote a post from here that’s on Bluesky too, and Bluesky shows its copy there inside yours.

![The same quote on Bluesky: Rastin Mehr's post, with the Anahita.io post and its picture shown inside it](/images/blog/quote-repost-bsky.webp "On Bluesky")

## Coming in

Quotes from the fediverse or Bluesky are presented in Anahita as quotes, with the original post displayed within them whenever the author has approved them or quoted themselves.  As with all content Anahita shares with other networks, participation is voluntary: your posts go to the fediverse or Bluesky only if you’ve chosen to be there. Sharing is an agreement, not an assumption.
